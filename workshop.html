Live Interactive Case Study (DEMO-001)

Inside "Your Wealth Snapshot™" — Jordan's 90-Day Pivot

Live Demo Profile (Jordan Morgan): $4,000/mo Net Pay • $2,820 Essential Bills • $480 Discretionary • $570 Debt Minimums • $130/mo Uncommitted Margin

Why Traditional Advice Backfires

  • With only $400 in savings and $130/mo margin, throwing extra cash at credit cards leaves Jordan vulnerable to unbudgeted irregular expenses.
  • One unexpected repair forces Jordan right back onto high-interest credit cards—triggering stress and financial avoidance.

The 90-Day Practical Defense

  • Days 1–30: Freeze card usage; pay minimums; automate $130/mo surplus to a separate savings account ($790 baseline by Day 90).
  • Days 31–60: Trim $70/mo from discretionary takeout to hit the $1,000 Buffer Goal in 90 days.
  • Day 91+: Pivot surplus into systematic debt elimination.

Risk Management & Tax Efficiency

Protecting Your Paycheck & Cash Flow

Smart Risk Management

  • Retain Small Risks, Insure Major Losses: Using appropriate deductibles keeps monthly insurance premiums affordable.
  • Protect Your Paycheck (Disability): Ensuring you have adequate income protection if illness or injury prevents you from working.
  • Liability Defense: Reviewing Homeowners/Renters and Auto liability limits, and adding an Umbrella policy when personal assets grow.

Tax & Withholding Efficiency

  • Avoid Unnecessary Tax Refunds: A massive spring tax refund means over-withholding all year while paying interest on credit cards[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
  • Pre-Tax Advantages: Utilizing workplace pre-tax retirement accounts or health savings options to lower your annual taxable income[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span).
