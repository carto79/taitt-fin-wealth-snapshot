## Overview
**Your Wealth Snapshot™** is a client-side web application and 90-day workplace financial wellness program developed by **Taitt Fin**. It integrates three rigorous disciplines into a single shame-free employee experience:
1. **The Clinical & Systems Lens (CFT™):** Systems Theory, Cybernetic feedback loops, Klontz Money Scripts, Financial Enmeshment boundaries, and the ABC-X Family Stress Model.
2. **The Behavioral Change Lens (Financial Social Work):** Transtheoretical Stages of Change, Readiness Ruler (0–10), healing "The Money Disconnect," the Personal Spending & Savings Plan (PSSP), and the Wolfsohn 3-Step Debt Solution.
3. **The Technical Planning Lens (CFP® Education & AFC®):** Starter emergency buffer defense, high-interest debt math, Risk Management Matrix (deductibles, disability, liability), marginal tax efficiency, inflation-adjusted retirement needs analysis, and essential estate/incapacity directives.

## Zero-Server Privacy Firewall
* **100% Client-Side Execution:** All scoring, peer normalization, cash flow simulations, and CFP® retirement calculations run locally inside the user's browser (`index.html`).
* **No Cloud Database:** No personally identifiable information (PII) or financial figures are transmitted to an external server. Users can save/export their own encrypted or local `.json` snapshot file and print directly to PDF.
